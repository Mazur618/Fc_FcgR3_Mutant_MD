Local interface changes are clearer than mutant energy rankings

Most mutants cannot be reliably ordered by whole-partner MM/GBSA with three short replicates. D265A has the clearest unfavorable shift, while selected protein and glycan contacts change consistently across tested settings.

8 systems · 3 × 10 ns per system · 140 analyzed snapshots per run · stored times 3.16–10.11 ns

The independent sample size is three trajectories per system. No mutant comparison passes the seven-comparison Holm-adjusted 0.05 threshold. This does not establish equivalence.

03 · Whole-partner MM/GBSA

D265A shifts unfavorably; other rankings remain uncertain

D265A − WT: +10.02 kcal/mol; unadjusted Welch 95% interval +5.30 to +14.74; Holm-adjusted p = 0.0645. Every other mutant’s unadjusted interval crosses zero.

For D265A, direct electrostatics (+148.40 kcal/mol) and GB solvation (-141.01) largely offset, leaving +7.39 kcal/mol net polar change.

Entropy is omitted. These are whole-partner estimates, not measured affinities, Kd folds, or an energy decomposition of a particular residue pair.

Scope of this exploration

Short runs, fixed protonation, truncated receptor glycans, and the chosen force field/solvent model limit interpretation. Imaging validation remains incomplete; legacy BSA is excluded because of invalid values. The original WT minimization reached its cycle limit without meeting its gradient threshold. See the repository provenance and reproduction guides for details.
